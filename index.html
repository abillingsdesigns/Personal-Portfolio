As a Designer

I specialize in product development and design, logo creation, brand identity, and print design. I currently work as the lead graphic artist and illustrator for Launch Marketing, designing for companies like Walmart, Target, and Sam's club. In addition, I work as a freelance designer for small businesses and contract-based projects. My designs combine fresh, unique modernity with classic compositions. I strive to provide each individual or company with work that visually represents not only the main surface of the brand, but the undertones and core values of it as well.

As an Artist

Most of my work lives in the realm of surrealism. I enjoy altering the regular to create something obsucre, otherworldly, and intriguing. I mainly work with charcoal, oils, and pencil- oftentimes using a blend of 3-4 media. My work has been featured in a couple of local galleries in NWA and I'm actively doing custom freelance commissions. In combination with my work as a designer, I love to illustrate! I have a wide variety of experience working in all kinds of printed media with multiple different styles. Whether for a custom greeting card or key component of your brand identity, incorporating illustration is a great way to add character.
