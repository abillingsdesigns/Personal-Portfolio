About Me

I'm Annabelle, or AB. I'm a designer and artist based in Northwest Arkansas. I'm self taught as an artist but currently pursuing my BFA in graphic design here in AR. I love all kinds of media, but am particularly drawn to oil paints, acrylic, and charcoal. Surrealism is my go-to, but I also enjoy realism with stylistic twists. My pieces range from bright, bold, whimsical colors in paintings to totally black and white with my charcoal work. Aside from fine art, I am the lead graphic artist at Launch Marketing, working with large-scale companies to create new, exciting designs in print, packaging, and digital media. I also work with local businesses in branding identity and design. I'm so excited that you stumbled across my work and I can't wait to see where this journey takes us.
